Business priority

Rules the simulator applies

Service gateNo option may add more than 5 transit days.
Carbon gateA route or port change may not raise CO₂e, even when it is cheaper.
Carbon price checkAn option that costs more than the internal price (US$40 ≈ ₹3,360 per t avoided) is parked unless it is part of a package that saves money.
Balanced scoreCarbon 35 · cost 25 · transit 10, each scaled best = 1 within the lane. Reliability and data confidence are applied as gates.
